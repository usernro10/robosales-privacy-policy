RoboSales Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

RoboSales is an automation service designed to help businesses manage and respond to customer inquiries received through WhatsApp.

2. Information We Process

RoboSales may process the following information:

  • Customer name
  • WhatsApp phone number
  • Messages sent by customers
  • Information related to service requests
  • Lead classification and customer service information
  • Technical information necessary to operate the service

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to process customer inquiries, classify leads, provide automated responses, organize customer requests, and help businesses manage their customer communications.

4. Third-Party Services

RoboSales may use third-party services including Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, Google services, and artificial intelligence services to process and respond to customer communications.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers only when necessary to provide the RoboSales service.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. User Rights

Customers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable laws and legitimate business requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the RoboSales service administrator.
