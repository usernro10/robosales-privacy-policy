RoboSales Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

RoboSales is an automation system designed to help businesses receive, classify, manage, and respond to customer inquiries through WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you interact with a business using RoboSales.

2. Information We Process

Depending on your interaction with the business, RoboSales may process:

  • Your name or WhatsApp profile name.
  • Your WhatsApp phone number.
  • Messages and information that you voluntarily send.
  • Information related to your inquiry or service request.
  • Technical information required to process WhatsApp messages.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Receive and respond to customer inquiries.
  • Classify and prioritize customer leads.
  • Maintain customer service records.
  • Improve business communication and response times.
  • Provide automated responses requested by the business.

4. Third-Party Services

RoboSales may use third-party services to provide its functionality, including WhatsApp and Meta services, Google services, and artificial intelligence services used to process and classify messages.

Information processed by these services is handled according to their respective privacy policies and applicable terms.

5. Data Sharing

RoboSales does not sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the automation, communicate with customers, store business records, or provide requested services.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain the security of the system.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other actions regarding your personal information.

To exercise applicable rights or ask questions about the processing of your information, please contact the business that you communicated with through WhatsApp.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to RoboSales, applicable requirements, or the services used by the system. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the processing of your information, please contact the business operating the RoboSales automation through its available customer support channels.
